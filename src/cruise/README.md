# The cruise ship

On the coast, a walk from where you first arrive, there's a cruise pier, and
a cruise ship that comes and goes from it: the **Halcyon**, of Tidewater
Cruises, home port Meridian. She sails between here and Maui, 835 km away,
on a timetable everyone in a world shares (see *Sailing*, below). She's the
size of the real ones: 300 m long and 36 across, white, her lifeboats hung
along her sides under the promenade deck, her funnel raked aft in the line's
blue with a gold sun on it, a slide tower on her top deck with two
waterslides corkscrewing off it, one of them out over the sea. Her piers are
on the map with a little ship on their badges, saying when she's in and
when she sails, and listed in Routes under *cruise piers*; pick one and
press **go here**, and you're set down on the pier at the foot of the
gangway. Alongside, she sounds her horn at noon and
at five.

While she's alongside, walk up the gangway, through the door in her side,
and she's yours to wander (while she's away, there's a chain across it).
Her rooms are tall and airy, three and a half metres to the ceiling (the
grand rooms go up two or three decks), and dressed as a ship of hers is:
carpet with a gold trellis down the corridors and up the stairs, marble with
black keystones in the lobbies, the atrium and the Galleria, wood panelling
under a rail, wallpaper, coffered ceilings with their lights let into the
coffers and a cove of light round the tops of the walls; a claret carpet
with gold medallions in the dining room and the theatre, a carpet as loud as
they come in the casino, parquet in the café and the bar, velvet on the
theatre's walls. Heels click on the marble.

The first time you come aboard you're given a key card: one of the balcony
staterooms is yours, and the HUD leads you to it, by the lifts and along the
corridors.

Three cores go up through her, aft, midships and forward: on every deck a
lobby with four lifts and a staircase. The stairs go up two flights a deck,
tread by tread. The lifts you walk into:

- **A lift** opens as you come up to its doors, as if it had come for you.
  Step in, **click** the buttons, and **W** and **S** go up and down the decks
  (the HUD says what's on each); **click** again to go, or **right-click** to
  step back out. The doors close, it goes, and they open on the deck you
  chose. The midships cars have glass backs, onto the atrium.
- **A stateroom's door**: walk up to it and **click**. Most open (whoever's
  booked in is out); one with a *do not disturb* on its handle won't. Each
  has its own colours, and whatever its people have left about: suitcases, a
  robe over the chair, the bed made or not, a towel folded into a swan or an
  elephant or a monkey; a bottle on ice in the suites. Every one is grown
  from the world's seed, so everyone sharing a world finds the same room
  behind the same door. Yours is made up for you, with a welcome card and
  your key card on the desk.
- **The pools and hot tubs**: step up onto the rim, and in. Out of your depth
  you swim, as anywhere; the steps at the end of a pool, or a hot tub's
  bench, are the way out.
- **The slides**: climb the tower (three turns of stairs from the sun deck),
  walk up to the top of a slide, and **click**. The Corkscrew is an open red
  flume, out over her starboard side and round her stern; the Plunge a
  closed blue tube, twice round over her port side. Both end in the splash
  pool.

Your tools are put away while you're aboard. Close the game anywhere aboard
and you open there again.

## Sailing

She sails between **Port Angeles**, where people first arrive (the world
starts on the Olympic Peninsula, in the Hoh rain forest, and her pier is on
the Strait of Juan de Fuca to the north), and **Kahului, on Maui**, where the
hikes round the island are. By the clock on the wall, as the trains run: everyone in a world sees
her in the same place, so you sail with whoever's aboard when she goes, and
if you're ashore when she sails, you've missed the boat (there's always the
plane: see [Airports](../airports/README.md)).

| | |
|---|---|
| alongside | 12 minutes at each port; the call for all aboard goes out 5 minutes before she sails, and her horn as she goes |
| leaving | 4 minutes: she moves off the pier sideways, swings round to point out to sea, and heads out along her lane, gathering way |
| at sea | 9 minutes: out until she's out of sight of land; then a fog bank comes down over her, and in the thick of it she's carried across the world, the way the plane is carried in cloud; then on, in toward the other port |
| arriving | 4 minutes: slowing, swinging round to lie along the pier, and onto it sideways |

The round trip is 58 minutes. Aboard, she carries you: walk her decks, take
the lifts, swim in the pools while the coast goes by, and she takes you
with her. Her wake spreads out behind her, her engines rumble, the wind
blows over her open decks; in the fog she sounds her horn every half
minute. The pier and the HUD say when she's due and when she sails; aboard,
you're told as she leaves, as she's out at sea, as she comes in, and when
she's alongside, until when.

**Her piers** are on the real coast (the real Earth's: see
[the world](../world/README.md#the-real-earth)), and where on it is grown
from the seed and the land (`site.js`). At home: along a ring of lines out
from the start, the first water each reaches, and from there straight out,
the nearest spot with water deep enough under her and round her, a short
pier out to it, and a lane straight out from it to the open sea, deep all
the way, with room to swing her round and no land in sight at its end;
clear of the old places, the towns, the caves, the railway, the launch
sites, the video stores, the abandoned buildings, the hunt clubs and the
airfield. At Maui the same, looking all the way round the hikes there, off
their land. Everyone in a world finds them in the same place.
The home pier is found once the airfields in its way have been (a moment
after the world opens), and Maui's a little after that. **Nothing about the
land changes for them**: the trestle starts where the ground is as high as
its deck and stands on piles from there; the trees in its way are hidden as
it comes into view.

What's solid aboard her is all of it, near her: her decks are rooms on
paper (`plan.js`), and every floor, wall, tread and rail is a solid. Only
the decks round you are drawn, and the staterooms near you furnished. The
pools are water held up in something (`world/basins.js`), which anyone may
use for a pool, a tub or a fountain. Under way, what's solid of her stays at
the berth she left, and you're carried (`player.deck`, a floor that moves:
you go about where she's solid and are put where that is on her now); so
she's solid only while she's alongside, or for you while you're aboard.

From the console:

```js
world.cruise.site            // her home pier
world.cruise.berths          // both piers (Maui's once it's been found)
world.cruise.voyage          // where she is in her round: phase, port, bound for, how long left
world.cruise.soon('sails', 5)  // move the clock: she sails in 5 s ('arrives', 'home', 'fog' too)
await world.cruise.go(1)     // stand at the foot of a pier's gangway (0 home, 1 Maui)
await world.cruise.board()   // ...or just inside the door in her side
world.cruise.where           // which deck you're on, and in what
world.cruise.yours           // your stateroom
world.cruise.toDeck(14)      // stand in a lobby on deck 14
world.cruise.toCabin(9142)   // in the corridor outside a stateroom's door
world.cruise.toSlide('plunge') // at the top of a slide
world.cruise.hurry()         // skip to the end of a lift ride, or a slide
```
